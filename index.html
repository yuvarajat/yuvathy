Before we say hello

A few things you
might be wondering.

Something else on your mind? Just ask. We’re happy to talk it through.

I have an idea, but no technical brief. Is that okay?

Yes. Start with the problem you want to solve. A description of your current workflow, a spreadsheet, or a few notes is enough to begin a conversation. We can work out the technical details together.

Can you help with software we already use?

We offer maintenance, integrations, and improvements to existing applications. Tell us what you use and what isn’t working; we’ll review it and discuss what’s practical.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?

That depends on the work. Once we understand what you need, we’ll discuss the scope, an estimate, and the likely timeline. If there’s a budget or deadline in mind, share it early so we can plan around it.

Do I need software to use your business services?

No. You can work with us on bookkeeping, registrations, or compliance support separately. See our accounting and compliance services for details. Specialist matters are coordinated with qualified professionals.
