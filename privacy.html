Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Yuvathy Technologies may receive through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Effective 1 September 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to yuvathy.in and communications initiated through this website. Separate products, client projects or services may have their own privacy terms or contractual data-processing provisions.

In this policy, “Yuvathy Technologies”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Yuvathy Technologies, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

2. Information we may collect

Information you provide

  • Your name, email address, phone number and company details.
  • Project requirements, messages and other information you choose to include in an enquiry.
  • Communications and records created when you correspond with us.

Technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic or security logs. We currently do not intentionally use advertising trackers on this website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, discuss projects and provide requested information.
  • To prepare proposals, deliver services and manage business relationships.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website and services.
  • To maintain appropriate business, legal and security records.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

We process personal information with consent, for specified legitimate uses, to take steps you request, to perform an agreement or where another lawful basis is available under applicable law.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted hosting, infrastructure, communications or professional-service providers that support our operations, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security expectations.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect legal rights or security, or as part of a genuine business restructuring. Some providers may process information outside your state or country, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including business, contractual, legal, accounting and security needs. Retention periods vary according to the nature and context of the information.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask us to provide information about our processing, correct or erase personal data, withdraw consent, or address a grievance. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

This business-focused website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from anyone under 18 through it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

8. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or grievances, email hello@yuvathy.in or write to Yuvathy Technologies, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Please include enough detail for us to understand and respond to your request.
