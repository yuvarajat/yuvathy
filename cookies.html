Cookie Policy

A clear summary of browser storage and external resources used by the Yuvathy Technologies website.

Effective 8 September 2026
On this page About cookies Current use Third parties Your choices Updates

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or service can ask a browser to store. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and cached resources. They may support essential functionality, preferences, security, measurement or advertising.

2. Our current use

Yuvathy Technologies uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website. Its JavaScript tag uses first-party cookies, including _ga and a property-specific _ga_<container-id> cookie, to distinguish visitors and maintain session information. We do not intentionally place advertising cookies through this website.

The public website does not provide user accounts or store website preferences in local storage. Our hosting and security infrastructure may also use essential technical mechanisms or process request logs to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain availability. Browser caching may retain copies of website files to improve performance.

3. Third-party resources

This website loads Google Analytics from Google. Fonts and interface icons are served directly with the website. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details, requested page or resource, interactions and timestamp. Its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy practices.

Links to other websites, including our product websites, may use different cookies or technologies. Please review the policies shown on those services.

4. Your browser choices

Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies and other site data. Blocking analytics cookies may reduce our ability to measure website usage but should not prevent access to the public pages. Browser help or privacy settings provide instructions specific to your device.

5. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when website technologies or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@yuvathy.in. For broader information about personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.

Analytics settings and consent controls should be reviewed periodically against applicable legal requirements and the locations where this website is offered.
