Cookie Policy

A clear summary of browser storage and external resources used by the Yuvathy Technologies website.

Effective 1 September 2026
On this page About cookies Current use Third parties Your choices Updates

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website or service can ask a browser to store. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and cached resources. They may support essential functionality, preferences, security, measurement or advertising.

2. Our current use

Yuvathy Technologies does not currently and intentionally place advertising or behavioural-analytics cookies through this website. The public website does not provide user accounts or store website preferences in local storage.

Our hosting and security infrastructure may use essential technical mechanisms or process request logs to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain availability. Browser caching may retain copies of website files to improve performance.

3. Third-party resources

This website currently requests fonts from Google Fonts and interface icons from jsDelivr. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested file and timestamp. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices.

Links to other websites, including our product websites, may use different cookies or technologies. Please review the policies shown on those services.

4. Your browser choices

Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies and other site data. Blocking essential storage may affect how some websites operate. Browser help or privacy settings provide instructions specific to your device.

Because this website currently does not intentionally deploy non-essential analytics or advertising cookies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner. If that changes, we will update this policy and provide choices where required.

5. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when website technologies or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@yuvathy.in. For broader information about personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.

Before introducing analytics, advertising, embedded media or additional third-party tools, this page and any consent mechanism should be reassessed.
